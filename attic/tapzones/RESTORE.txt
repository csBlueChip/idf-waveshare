Tap zones - shelved at 0.7.58
=============================

Removed from the firmware at 0.7.58; last built in 0.7.57 (full sources:
wavedemo (57).zip - easiest to diff src/imu.c, src/nv.c against it).
Results and why: results.txt.

                      / _PFX / _EXT / _FILE_MAX), zone + capture statics,
                      imuTapVal_t, capture (imuCapWanted, imuCapDrain, ring),
